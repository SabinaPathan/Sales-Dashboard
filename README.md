# Executive Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive sales dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze revenue, profitability, sales trends, product categories, sales channels, and country-level performance.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming sales data into meaningful business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard. It provides an executive-level overview of key performance indicators (KPIs) and helps users explore sales performance across different dimensions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall revenue, gross profit, gross margin, quantity sold, and total orders.
- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify the top-performing and bottom-performing countries by revenue.
- Understand sales distribution across product categories and sales channels.
- Explore sales performance using interactive filters.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** — Dataset inspection and data handling
- **DAX** — For calculated measures, where applicable

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays five key metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 645.22K |
| Total Gross Profit | 296.38K |
| Gross Margin | 45.94% |
| Total Quantity Sold | 6,698 |
| Total Orders | 2,707 |

### 2. Monthly Sales Trends

A line chart compares monthly revenue and profit, helping users explore changes in sales performance over time.

### 3. Country Performance Analysis

- **Top 5 Countries:** Highlights countries generating the highest revenue.
- **Bottom 5 Countries:** Identifies countries with comparatively lower revenue.
- **Geographic Map:** Provides a visual overview of sales performance across countries.

### 4. Product Category Analysis

Pie or donut charts show how sales are distributed across product categories, helping users compare category contributions.

### 5. Sales Channel Analysis

Visualizes the distribution of sales across available channels to help compare their relative contributions.

### 6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Quarter
- Month
- Product Category
- Country

These filters allow users to explore specific segments and compare performance dynamically.

## Key Insights

Based on the dashboard's displayed KPIs and visualizations:

- The dashboard reports total revenue of **645.22K** and gross profit of **296.38K**.
- The reported gross margin is **45.94%**, providing an overview of profitability relative to revenue.
- The dashboard tracks **6,698 units sold** across **2,707 orders**.
- Country-level rankings help distinguish higher-revenue and lower-revenue markets.
- Monthly trend analysis supports the examination of changes in revenue and profit over time.
- Product category and sales channel visualizations help compare sales composition.
